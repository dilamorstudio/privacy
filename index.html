Dilamor Glyko Edge – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Glyko Edge is currently in beta. This policy may change before the public release.

1. Who we are

Dilamor Glyko Edge is a watch face made by Dilamor Studio, Slovakia ("we", "us"). Contact: dilamorstudio@gmail.com.

2. What the watch face does

Glyko Edge shows the most recent glucose reading from the sensor data service you already use, together with a trend arrow, the unit and the age of the reading. The watch does not measure glucose. Glyko Edge is a secondary display only. It is not intended for diagnosis or treatment decisions and provides no alarms or alerts. Readings may be delayed or missing.

3. Data the watch face handles

DataWhere it is keptWhy
Account username and password for your sensor data service, and the account region you chooseApp settings of the Garmin Connect IQ platform (on your watch, and on your phone where you enter them)To sign in on your behalf and fetch your reading
A protected copy of your username and password, together with the key that protects itApp storage on your watchSo the background refresh can check that your settings did not change before it signs in
A sign-in session identifier issued by your provider, and a random internal tokenApp storage on your watchTo avoid signing in for every refresh
Latest glucose value, trend, unit, and the time of the readingApp storage on your watchTo show the reading

We do not keep a history of readings. We do not collect or send analytics, advertising identifiers, location or usage data.

4. Where the data goes

  • About every five minutes the watch face asks your sensor data service for your latest reading over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Your username and password are sent to that service so that it can sign you in. The request is made by the Garmin Connect IQ platform on your behalf.
  • Depending on the region you choose, the request goes to servers of your provider in the United States, outside the United States, or in Japan.
  • Your sensor data service is the Dexcom Share service. Through the Garmin Connect IQ platform and your phone, your watch sends your username and password to Dexcom's Share servers in the region you choose and receives your latest reading. Dexcom processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy. Dilamor Studio is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Dexcom, and Dexcom has not reviewed or approved Glyko Edge. Dexcom and Dexcom Share are trademarks of their owner and are used here only to identify the service. Enter only the account details of that service.
  • We do not operate a server for Glyko Edge. Your username, password and readings are never sent to Dilamor Studio.
  • Garmin and your phone may keep copies of app settings as part of their own services. See their privacy policies.

5. Why we process it and on what basis

To show you your own reading, which you request by entering your credentials and ticking the acknowledgement in the app settings. Glucose data is health data.

6. How long the data is kept and how you delete it

  • Username and password stay until you choose Withdraw acknowledgement and clear credentials or Forget account in the watch face settings. Forget account also resets the region.
  • Session and last reading are removed when you use either of those actions. In addition they are cleared every time the watch face starts and after any error, so they are kept only until the next restart.
  • Either action removes the stored credentials, the sign-in session and the last reading from the watch. If you choose it while another watch face is active, it is applied the next time Glyko Edge starts, and nothing is sent in the meantime.
  • Deleting data on the watch does not delete data held by your sensor data service, by Garmin, or copies held by your phone. Use their own tools for that.
  • Before removing the watch face, use Forget account while Glyko Edge is your active watch face. We cannot confirm that removing the watch face deletes the stored data.

7. Security

Requests use HTTPS. Your password is stored in the platform app settings in readable form on your device, and a second copy in app storage is encrypted with a key that is stored right next to it. This protects against accidental or inconsistent changes, not against someone who can read the device storage. Use a strong, unique password.

8. Your rights

If GDPR applies to you, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your personal data, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to a supervisory authority. In Slovakia that is the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR). Because we do not receive your data, we usually cannot look it up; write to us and we will explain what we can do.

9. Children

Glyko Edge is not directed at children.
